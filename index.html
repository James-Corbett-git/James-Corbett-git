About Me

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics & Statistics at Stony Brook University, advised by Roman Samulyak. My work centers on the numerical simulation of processes related to fueling and disruption mitigation in nuclear fusion reactors such as tokamaks and stellarators.

In practice, I am often building and extending software in C++ designed to solve PDE & ODE systems. I have held roles in other domains in the past, including an internship at Zencastr where I developed in Python supporting the machine learning team, and at KPMG and Tapestry where I worked in Transfer Pricing.

Research Focus

Pellet Fueling - Low magnetic Reynolds number Magnetohydrodynamics

3D simulation of pellet ablation clouds sheds light on quantities of interest including the ablation rate and rocket acceleration which affect how injected pellets distribute mass and impurities in fusion plasmas. Model ablation cloud flow in the low magnetic Reynolds number approximation.

Rocket Acceleration & Disruption Mitigation

Developed software to model plasma response to ablating fragments in order to estimate impact of rocket acceleration on ablated material deposition.

Striation Instability

Working towards demonstrating the striation instability in 3D simulations of pellet ablation clouds.

Software Development

PELOTON Contributor

Simulation Codebase

PELOTON is a parallelized C++ software designed to simulate gas-plasma flow governed by magnetohydrodynamic equations in the low magnetic Reynolds number approximation.

  • I leveraged Stony Brook's SeaWulf cluster to produce simulations across a large paramater ranges to tabulate quantities of interest such as the ablation rate and rocket acceleration.
  • Participated in code improvement, expansion, and testing efforts.
  • Member of ongoing development of one-dimensional plasmoid expansion solver.
  • Expanding modelling capabilities to explore striation instability phenomenon.
C++MPIp4estHydro Solvers

PELOTON Cooling Code

Simulation Codebase

Developed C++ software to simulate plasma dynamics during fueling & shatter pellet injection, a technique used for disruption mitigation.

  • Applied software to validate PELOTON rocket acceleration results by comparison with experiment in JET.
  • Model supports dilution cooling, ohmic heating of electrons, ion-electron energy exchange, radiation energy loss, and charge state evolution.
  • Gained experience with multi-scale modelling and simulation, scientific software design, and C++ programming.
